Biophotonics Lab

Nanoscale Plasmonics, SERS Biosensing & Optical Neural Interfaces

We harness the unique optical properties of nanoscale plasmonic materials and structures to develop integrated platforms for biosensing, early disease diagnostics, bioimaging, and neural engineering—bridging biomedical engineering, optics, electronics, and nanobiotechnology.

Research Areas

Integrated design, fabrication, and optical characterization of nanoscale plasmonic systems

01 / RAMAN & EARLY DIAGNOSTICS

SERS-Based Early Disease Diagnostics

Surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS) biosensors and plasmonic immunoassay platforms for rapid, ultrasensitive detection of clinical biomarkers and early-stage diseases.

02 / PLASMONIC NANOSTRUCTURES

Nanostructure Design & SPR Sensing

Electromagnetic modeling and fabrication of metallic nanograting, nanocrown, and nanoparticle array substrates to maximize localized surface plasmon resonance sensitivity.

03 / BIOIMAGING & NEURAL ENGINEERING

Nanoscale Imaging & Neural Interfaces

Plasmon-enhanced optical neural stimulation and high-resolution nanoscale bioimaging systems integrating photonics with biomedical electronics.
