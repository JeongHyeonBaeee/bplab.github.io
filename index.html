Welcome to Biophotonics Lab

Welcome to the Biophotonics Laboratory at Kyung Hee University, directed by Prof. Kyung Min Byun. Our research focuses on understanding light–matter interactions at the nanoscale and translating plasmonic and optical technologies into high-sensitivity biomedical diagnostics and healthcare platforms.

Research Areas

Core interdisciplinary research themes in nanophotonics, biosensing, and medical devices.

Research Theme 01

Plasmonic Nanostructures & SERS Biosensors

We design and fabricate cost-effective, large-area plasmonic substrates—such as convective self-assembled nanoparticles, metallic nanogaps, and porous biomaterials (silk fibroin, nitrocellulose, chitosan)—to achieve ultra-sensitive Surface-Enhanced Raman Spectroscopy (SERS) and Localized Surface Plasmon Resonance (LSPR) detection of disease biomarkers, glucose, and pathogens.

Research Theme 02

Fiber-Optic & Miniaturized Point-of-Care Optical Systems

To translate optical spectroscopy from the benchtop to clinical and field settings, we develop hollow-core fiber SERS probes, 3D-printed multimodal Raman probes, and portable optical readers capable of rapid, label-free molecular diagnosis with high signal-to-noise ratios.

Research Theme 03

Optical Neuromodulation & Wearable Biomedical Devices

We explore near-infrared photothermal neural stimulation mediated by gold nanorods as well as flexible, sustainable wearable sensors—including upcycled chitosan-based electronic skin, digital stethoscopes, and pulse oximetry imaging systems—for continuous physiological monitoring and human–machine interfaces.
