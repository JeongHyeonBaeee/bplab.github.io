About Our Lab

At the Biophotonics Lab, we harness the unique optical properties of nanoscale plasmonic materials and structures to develop integrated platforms for biosensing, early disease diagnostics, bioimaging, and neural engineering.

Combining biomedical engineering, optics, electronics, and nanobiotechnology, our research spans the full cycle from nanostructure design and fabrication to optical characterization.

Research Areas

Multidisciplinary integration of nanophotonics, biomedical engineering, and electronics

01 / RAMAN & EARLY DIAGNOSTICS

SERS-Based Early Disease Diagnostics

Surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS) biosensors and sandwich immunoassay platforms for rapid, ultrasensitive detection of clinical biomarkers and early-stage diseases.

02 / PLASMONIC NANOSTRUCTURES

Nanostructure Design & SPR Sensing

Electromagnetic simulation (FDTD) and convective self-assembly (CSA) fabrication of metallic nanogap, nanograting, and nanocrown substrates to maximize plasmonic sensitivity.

03 / BIOIMAGING & NEURAL ENGINEERING

Nanoscale Bioimaging & Neural Interfaces

Plasmon-enhanced optical neural stimulation, non-invasive neuromodulation, and wearable biophotonic interfaces bridging photonics with biomedical electronics.
