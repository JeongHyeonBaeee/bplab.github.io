Biophotonics Lab

Nanoscale Plasmonics, SERS Biosensing & Optical Neural Interfaces

We harness the unique optical properties of nanoscale plasmonic materials and structures to develop integrated platforms for ultrasensitive biosensing, medical diagnostics, bioimaging, and neural engineering.

Explore Research Join Our Lab

Research Areas

Multidisciplinary integration of nanophotonics, biomedical engineering, and electronics

01 / NANOPLASMONICS

Plasmonic Nanostructures & Self-Assembly

Design, electromagnetic simulation (FDTD), and convective self-assembly (CSA) fabrication of high-density metallic nanogap arrays for reproducible localized hotspot generation.

02 / BIOSENSING & DIAGNOSTICS

SERS & SPR Immunoassay Platforms

Ultrasensitive surface-enhanced Raman scattering (SERS) sandwich biosensors for trace biomarker profiling and early clinical screening of sepsis and inflammatory diseases.

03 / BIOIMAGING & NEURAL

Nanoscale Bioimaging & Neural Interfaces

High-resolution plasmonic bioimaging and optical neural interface systems bridging photonics and electrophysiology for real-time cellular monitoring.
